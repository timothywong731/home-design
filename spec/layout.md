# Ground-floor plan layout (global topology + dimensions)

Source: 13 contact sheets, ~250 individual frames, ~40 extra full-res stills (in `reference/work/layout/`).
`reference/transcript.txt` appeared after the plan was derived; it was then checked and agrees with it:
"go straight and make a right, I enter the kitchen" (f_0285), "mini bar on the left hand side" (f_0311),
"a door towards the garden" (f_0328, kitchen back door), "a window by the back" (f_0483-0490, utility),
"behind here there is a toilet" + "sliding door" + "two doors here" (f_0492-0538), "the windows are
symmetrical ... identical window on each side" (f_0667-0689, dining). The owner calls the hall arch a
"half arch" (f_0272) and the vestibule "the porch" with "corners" (the piers) and "an arch" on top.
The narration gives no dimensions.

Coordinates follow BRIEF: metres, Z up, origin = centre of inside face of the front-door threshold,
+Y into the house, +X to the right walking in. "North" below is shorthand for +Y (rear), "east" for +X.
All rectangles are **internal clear faces**. Tolerance is about +/-0.15 m on room sizes and +/-0.3 m on
cumulative Y positions (the lens is a heavily distorted ultrawide; every dimension comes from counting
things or from local ratios against a door leaf, never from long-range perspective).

## 0. Headline findings (several differ from the BRIEF prior)

1. **No level changes.** The kitchen is NOT stepped down: tiles run flush to a flat threshold bar
   (stills t=437 s, t=754.5 s, f_0439). What looks like steps in dark frames f_0288/f_0293 is grout lines.
   Vestibule tiles, hall oak, kitchen/utility/WC tiles and dining oak are all Z = 0 (+/-10 mm thresholds).
2. **Everything is on the +X (right) side of the hall.** The hall runs along the -X party wall for the whole
   depth of the house. Order front to rear on the right: living room, staircase (inside the hall width),
   kitchen, WC, utility. The dining room closes the end of the hall and spans the full back-addition width.
3. **The hall changes width.** Front hall ~1.55 m wide. Staircase (0.82) takes its east side leaving a 0.73 m
   passage. After the stairs it is full width again for ~1.05 m (kitchen-door lobby), then the corbelled
   **arch** (the original rear wall of the main house) and beyond it a 1.00 m corridor in the back addition.
4. **The kitchen is the old rear reception room** (main house body), U-shaped units, with the white back
   door in its north wall at the east end, opening onto a side-return yard beside the back addition.
5. **The WC sits between kitchen and utility**, entered only from the utility through an oak pocket door.
6. **The living-room bay's west cheek window opens into the vestibule** (seen from both sides: f_0019-0021
   from the vestibule; f_0211-0213, f_0127 from the living room). The vestibule is the old open porch,
   enclosed by the white outer doors in line with the bay front.

## 4. Staircase

- Footprint x -0.045..0.775 (0.82 wide over the string), y 4.25..7.85. Starts 0.15 m north of the living
  door north architrave. Climbs toward **+Y** (rear). Wall on its east side (the hall east wall, with a
  raking dado rail); open balustrade on the west side (mahogany handrail + newel, white turned spindles,
  cut string with brackets, panelled spandrel below).
- **16 risers** of ~0.197 (floor to floor ~3.15 m): 14 nosings are countable in stills hr_241/hr_243 plus
  the top landing riser; counted range 15-16.
- Steps 1-3 are curved (bullnose/curtail, slightly fanned, not true winders): step 1 wraps west around the
  foot of the newel to about x = -0.25; goings ~0.27 on the walking line. Newel at about (-0.045, 4.55).
- Steps 4-15: 12 straight goings of ~0.235.
- Top riser at about y 7.85, directly over the under-stairs door wall. The first-floor landing continues
  north over the lobby. Model the flight up to the first-floor opening and stop.
- No winders at the top; the flight is straight in plan.

- Stair run 3.60: 3 curved goings + 12 straight goings.
- Vestibule 1.45 x 1.75: 12.5 x 13.6 tile cells, cell size set by the width matching the inner frame;
  depth nudged up toward the door-height estimate.
- Living 3.6 x 4.1: north wall furniture run (bookcase ~1.8 + margins), east wall run (sofa ~2.1, table,
  fan-coil), TV wall ~2.5 from door architrave to bay pier.
- Kitchen 3.6 x 4.0: unit modules (east: 0.55 gap + 0.6 + 0.9 + 0.3 + corner; west: corner + ovens 0.6 +
  fridge housing 1.0 + 0.4 + door 0.92) and the constraint that it must span from the living north wall to
  the arch wall alongside the 3.6 m stair plus the 1.05 m lobby.
- Utility 2.675 x 1.95: two 0.6 machines + 0.6 sink + 0.6 return on the north wall; door + worktop depth on

- Ceilings: inner-door frame top at 2.91 by door-height scale (hr_057) gives ~2.9 in the hall; dining 2.8

## 7. Uncertainties (best guess already used above)

1. **Absolute Y of everything north of the living door: +/-0.3 m.** The chain is vestibule 1.75 + wall 1.3
   + door + stair 3.6 + lobby 1.05. If narration or a real measurement gives any one length, shift the rest.
2. **Vestibule depth** 1.75 (tile count says 1.6, door-height perspective says 1.85) and with it the bay
   depth (0.70) and the position of the porch piers.
3. **Living-room depth** 4.1 (range 3.9-4.4) and **kitchen depth** 4.0 (range 3.7-4.3); they trade off
   against each other because their sum is pinned by the stair.
4. **East party wall position** x = 4.50 (room width 3.6, range 3.4-3.9). Living and kitchen are assumed to
   share it.
5. **Back-addition width** (flank wall at x = 3.00, range 2.8-3.3) and so the side-return width. The side
   return itself is never filmed; its existence is inferred from the kitchen back door, and the WC,
   utility and dining east windows.
6. **Dining length** 5.2 (range 4.8-5.8) and whether its west wall is exactly in line with the hall west
   wall (hr_741 suggests within 0.1 m).
7. **Nook**: modelled as a 0.45 deep recess in the old rear wall. It may instead be full-depth units
   between two built-out cheeks; the visible result is the same.
8. **WC length**: assumed to run the full 2.675 to the flank wall because it has a window; the pocket door
   position (centre x 1.40) is +/-0.2.
9. **Door swings marked "guess"**: front doors, kitchen back door, French doors (never opened on camera).
10. **Riser count** 16 (could be 15). First-floor level 3.15 (range 3.0-3.2).
11. **Ceiling heights** +/-0.1; utility/WC 2.45 is the least certain. Vestibule vault profile is approximate.
12. **Dining rear wall glazing**: side lights each side of the French doors are partly behind curtains and a
    cat tree; widths and sills are approximate. No chimney breast was identified for the dining fireplace.

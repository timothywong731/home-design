## 1. Shape and dimensions

end of the garden. The roof oversails the front by 1.6 m to make a covered veranda, closed at the west end
by a shallow brick store cupboard and at the east end by a brick wing wall.

## 3. Architecture and trim

- **Walls**: facing brick in stretcher bond, 75 mm coursing (door head = 27½ courses; window sill at
  course 14), flush joints. Front wall, wing wall, pier and store all the same brick.
- **Canopy**: the flat roof carries forward 1.60 over the veranda with no posts (the wing wall and SW pier
  support its ends). Soffit lined with **stained tongue-and-groove boards running E–W** (≈ 95 mm faces,
  V-jointed), with a continuous white plastic **soffit vent strip** (70 mm wide, slotted) 0.10 behind the
  front edge.
- **Fascia**: white uPVC board 0.25 deep along the whole front (world y 23.40, x −0.87–4.45) with a white
  half-round gutter 0.11 wide on brackets and a **white round downpipe** at the west end, dropping down
  the south face of the SW pier at world x ≈ −0.45 (v2:f_0191, f_0193, v4:f_0467).
- **Roof**: flat, grey mineral felt, falling ~70 mm to the front gutter; low kerb (0.12) with a grey
  coping along the west side and the back; at each front corner the brickwork rises as a short pier
  0.33 × 0.45 to 0.35 above the roof with a dark lead/felt cap (the east one is conspicuous in
  v2:f_0189, f_0203). Leaves and moss on the roof.
- **Wing wall** (east end of the veranda): its west face carries the coat-hook rail (§5); the grey garden
  fence butts its outer SE corner.
- No plinth, no string course. Paving runs to the brick.
- **Interior**: plastered and painted walls, flat plasterboard ceiling with a small plain coving
  (≈ 70 mm), no skirting visible (assume 70 mm white square-edge).

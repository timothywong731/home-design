## 12. Doubts
1. **Room size**: 2.40 × 1.75 is fixture arithmetic. Along the south wall roll-holder zone 0.47 + rail 0.50 +
   gap 0.05 + vanity 0.60 + gap 0.03 + bath 0.75 = 2.40; if the bath is 0.70 or the vanity 0.50 the room is 2.25–
   2.30 and the west wall is thicker. Length = bath length; an 1800 bath (and a 1200 tray) would make it 1.85.
2. **South wall position** (world y 10.91) follows from the length; what is behind it was not seen — the
   `landing1` / `yellow_room` specs must leave this rectangle free.
3. Ceiling height (2.50 assumed), downlight count.
4. Window: recess size ±0.05, depth 0.35–0.55 (depends on the flank-wall thickness: 0.20 in house.json, ≈ 0.35 in
   `garden`); z of the ledge 1.25–1.35; outward top-hung opening assumed from the handle position.
5. Shower: tray 1100 × 800 vs 1200 × 800; which pane slides (the roller pair is on the north pane in t358);
   exact x of the control on the back wall (±0.15).
6. Towel rail height 1.50 (1.2–1.6) and bar count; mirror size ±0.05.
7. Slab format of the marble cladding and the position of its joints.
8. Wall blue: HLG source, see the colour warning in `blue_bedroom`.
